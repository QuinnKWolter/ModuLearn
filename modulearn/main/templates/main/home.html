Start from the job

Three paths, one course model

ModuLearn is built around units and modules. The same structure can support a class session, a research study, or a small custom demo.

Teach

Create sessions, tune visibility and unlock rules, enroll students, and review recent activity without digging through separate tools.

Learn

Students see the sessions they belong to, open the next available module, and keep a clear record of progress.

Research

Studies can route participants into conditions, collect form responses, track module events, and export usable CSV data.
