About ModuLearn

ModuLearn is a small, open portal for putting smart-learning content into course sessions and research studies while keeping the local learning record readable.

Structures Launches Progress Exports

What it owns

The structure around the learning tool.

External modules still do the specialized teaching work. ModuLearn keeps their place in the course clear: who can see them, what unlocks next, what was completed, and what instructors or researchers can export later.

Useful today

  • Course sessionsStudents, units, modules, locks, and analytics.
  • Study sessionsParticipant links, condition assignment, consent, surveys, and exports.
  • Smart contentSPLICE, PCEX, PCRS, links, files, forms, and embedded activities.

Worth knowing

  • External modules may have their own language, login, iframe, or browser restrictions.
  • Legacy analytics depend on PAWS and Aggregate database services being reachable.
  • The English and Spanish toggle covers ModuLearn interface text, not third-party content inside modules.
