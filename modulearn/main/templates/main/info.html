About ModuLearn

ModuLearn is a Django-based portal for arranging interactive learning modules into reusable structures, live sessions, and research studies.

What the platform is responsible for

ModuLearn keeps the local learning record: course structures, session enrollment, module access, progress, form responses, focus events, study conditions, and exports. External tools still do the specialized teaching work; ModuLearn gives them a stable place in a course or study flow.

Useful today

  • Course sessionsReusable structures with students, units, modules, locks, and analytics
  • Study sessionsParticipant entry links, condition assignment, consent and survey modules
  • Embedded contentSPLICE content, PCEX, PCRS, forms, files, and ordinary external links
  • ExportsCourse and study data shaped for review outside the platform

Course model

A course structure contains units and modules. A session turns that structure into something students can join and complete.

Study model

A study owns participant-facing sessions, experimental conditions, Prolific entry links, and study-specific analytics.

Progress model

Module progress and event timelines preserve completion, scores, load events, focus changes, and tool callbacks when available.

Worth knowing

  • External modules may have their own language, login, iframe, or browser restrictions.
  • Legacy analytics depend on the PAWS and Aggregate database services being reachable.
  • The English and Spanish toggle covers ModuLearn interface text, not third-party content loaded inside modules.
